Installation Complete

ProSlideRelay has been installed to /Applications.

Getting started:

  1. Open ProPresenter → Preferences → Network and enable the Network API.
  2. Launch ProSlideRelay from Applications.
  3. Click the P7 icon in your menu bar → Settings and enter your ProPresenter port.
  4. Share the displayed URL with anyone on your Wi-Fi — they open it on their phone to follow along.
